# Helios Cloud Storage: Pricing Plans

Helios Cloud Storage offers three plans: Starter, Business, and Enterprise. All prices are in US dollars and billed monthly unless an annual contract is signed.

## Starter
The Starter plan costs $0.021 per GB-month of stored data. It includes 1 TB of free egress per month; additional egress is billed at $0.05 per GB. Starter accounts are limited to 5 buckets and 3 API keys. Starter has no uptime SLA and support is community-forum only.

## Business
The Business plan costs $0.018 per GB-month with a minimum monthly commitment of $250. It includes 5 TB of free egress per month; additional egress is billed at $0.03 per GB. Business accounts can create up to 100 buckets and 50 API keys. Business includes a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA and email support with a first-response target of 8 business hours.

## Enterprise
Enterprise pricing is negotiated per contract, with a typical list price of $0.015 per GB-month and a minimum annual commitment of $60,000. Enterprise includes unlimited buckets, unlimited API keys, a 99.99% monthly uptime SLA, a named technical account manager, and 24/7 phone support with a first-response target of 30 minutes for Severity 1 incidents.

## Annual discount
Business customers who prepay for 12 months receive a 12% discount on storage charges. The discount does not apply to egress charges or to the minimum commitment top-up.

## Free trial
New accounts receive a 30-day free trial with up to 100 GB of storage on Business-plan features. Trial accounts cannot enable cross-region replication.
