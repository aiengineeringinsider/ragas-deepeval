# Helios Cloud Storage: Service Level Agreement and Service Credits

## Measurement
Uptime is measured per region per calendar month as the percentage of 5-minute intervals in which the storage API returned successful responses to at least 99% of valid requests. Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance is excluded from the calculation.

## Credit schedule (Business plan, 99.9% SLA)
- Monthly uptime below 99.9% but at least 99.0%: 10% credit of that month's storage charges.
- Monthly uptime below 99.0% but at least 95.0%: 25% credit.
- Monthly uptime below 95.0%: 50% credit.

## Credit schedule (Enterprise plan, 99.99% SLA)
- Monthly uptime below 99.99% but at least 99.9%: 10% credit.
- Monthly uptime below 99.9% but at least 99.0%: 25% credit.
- Monthly uptime below 99.0%: 100% credit of that month's storage charges.

## Claiming credits
Customers must submit a credit request through the support portal within 30 days of the end of the affected month. Credits are applied to the next invoice and are never paid out in cash. Total credits in a month cannot exceed that month's storage charges. Egress charges are not eligible for credits.

## Exclusions
The SLA does not cover outages caused by customer misconfiguration, by exceeding documented rate limits, or by force majeure events. Starter plan accounts are not covered by any SLA.
