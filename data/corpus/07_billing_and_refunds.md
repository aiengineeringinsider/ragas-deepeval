# Helios Cloud Storage: Billing, Invoices, and Refunds

## Billing cycle
Usage is metered hourly and invoiced on the first day of each calendar month for the previous month. Invoices are payable within 30 days. Accepted payment methods are credit card (all plans) and bank transfer (Business and Enterprise only).

## Late payment
If an invoice is unpaid 15 days after its due date, the account is placed in read-only mode: existing data can be read and downloaded, but new uploads are blocked. If the invoice is still unpaid 45 days after the due date, the account is suspended and all API access is blocked. Data is not deleted during suspension; suspended accounts are closed after 90 days of non-payment.

## Refunds
Helios does not refund usage charges that have already been incurred. Annual prepayments can be refunded on a prorated basis only if the customer cancels within the first 30 days of the contract. Service credits under the SLA are not refunds and are applied to future invoices.

## Taxes
Prices exclude taxes. VAT is charged for customers in the European Union unless a valid VAT ID is provided. Customers in Indonesia are charged 11% VAT on digital services.

## Changing plans
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the current month. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Downgrading from Business to Starter is blocked while any bucket uses Object Lock or cross-region replication.
