# Helios Cloud Storage: Support Policy and Incident Severity

## Severity levels
- Severity 1: production storage is unavailable or data integrity is at risk, with no workaround.
- Severity 2: a major feature is degraded or performance is severely impacted, and a workaround exists.
- Severity 3: a minor issue or a general question that does not affect production.

## First-response targets
- Business (email support): 8 business hours for all severities.
- Enterprise (24/7 phone and email): 30 minutes for Severity 1, 2 hours for Severity 2, and 1 business day for Severity 3.
- Starter: community forum only, with no response target.

First-response targets are goals, not guarantees, and missing them does not create service credits.

## Status page and postmortems
Active incidents are posted on status.helios.example within 15 minutes of detection. For every Severity 1 incident, Helios publishes a written postmortem within 5 business days.

## Scheduled maintenance
Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 72 hours in advance and is performed between 02:00 and 05:00 local region time.
